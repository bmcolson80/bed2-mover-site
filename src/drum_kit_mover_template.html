Underlying plan

Studio walls (concept, unconfirmed)

Each wall is built from its existing stud face (after the plaster is stripped from the studio side) into the studio: sheets, or air gap then (optionally) a second stud wall and sheets. The dividing wall is built from the laundry end (bedroom face on the 200 step line, 2440 from the laundry wall) toward the studio. Where walls with air gaps meet, the gaps run into each other so the air gap is continuous round the studio. Colours: stud Soundchek air gap

Ceiling items (concept)

Fan sizes from Fanco's spec sheet: blade diameter 1220, canopy 170, ceiling to lowest blade 270, ceiling to lowest part of the light 330 (CCT LED model). The 340 square lined well is carried over from the v4 design (unconfirmed). Vent positions and duct routes follow v4 and are placeholders: ducts go through the roof, airflow and silencers are not drawn. Positions move with the studio clear area.

Options

Keys (click the box first): arrows nudge 10 mm (Shift 100 mm); [ and ] rotate 1° (Shift 15°); R rotates 90°.
The arrow inside the box marks which way the drummer faces, so you can see the throne side as you rotate.
Position readouts are always measured from the existing window wall and back wall inner faces, so they match across plans. Clearances are to the box extents. The box is only a footprint (no riser, no door swing).
