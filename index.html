Pick any base plan or design underneath, then drag the shaded box to move it and the round handle to rotate. The kit keeps its position (mm from the window and back walls) when you switch plans, so you can compare. 1:25 when printed at A3.

Studio walls (concept, unconfirmed)

Each wall is built from its existing face into the studio: sheets, or air gap then (optionally) a second stud wall and sheets. The dividing wall is built from the laundry end (bedroom face on the 200 step line, 2440 from the laundry wall) toward the studio. Existing studio walls are shown as stud with their faces unchanged (any plasterboard strip-back is not modelled). Colours: stud Soundchek air gap

Ceiling items (concept)

Placed in the studio, in the ceiling above the kit. Fan blade span is a placeholder until you give the model. Ventilation is your inline fans and sound mazes (not an ERV) and the 450 boxes only mark where the grilles might go; ducts and airflow are not drawn.

Kit size (mm)

Measured by Brendan on 15 Nov 2026: 1900 wide x 1600 deep. Enough to sit and play, not to walk around.

Options

Keys (click the box first): arrows nudge 10 mm (Shift 100 mm); [ and ] rotate 1° (Shift 15°); R rotates 90°.
The arrow inside the box marks which way the drummer faces, so you can see the throne side as you rotate.
Position readouts are always measured from the existing window wall and back wall inner faces, so they match across plans. Clearances are to the box extents. The box is only a footprint (no riser, no door swing).
